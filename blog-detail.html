Inside Stackly's 800V Solid-State Battery Breakthrough in Salem

Dr. Arun Kumar September 24, 2026 5 Min Read

At Stackly Automotive's 50-acre innovation campus in Salem, Tamil Nadu, our battery research team has reached a milestone: completing 10,000 thermal cycles on solid-state lithium cells operating under 800V fast-charge conditions.

"Solid-state battery chemistry eliminates volatile organic electrolytes. It allows us to deliver 520 miles range in the Stackly Aero EV while enabling 10-80% fast charging in under 12 minutes."

Traditional battery packs degrade significantly under tropical heat conditions. By utilizing ceramic solid electrolytes developed specifically for Indian climates, our Salem engineering team has reduced thermal degradation by over 95%.
